Who we are

Kromatch is run by [YOUR FULL NAME / BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], Greece ("we"). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. We are the data controller for the information described here.

The short version

  • You can use Kromatch without an account. Photos and designs you match stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • When you order from a shop, we share your name and contact details with that shop, only for that order and only with your consent.
  • Your location is only used on your phone to pick the nearest city. It is never stored or sent to us.
  • An account is optional. You can delete it, and your saved data, from Settings at any time.
  • If you allow it (on by default, can be switched off in Settings), Kromatch records anonymous colour data, such as which colours are matched and which cans are chosen, with your city. It never includes your name, email, photos or location. Summaries of this data may be shared with or sold to paint makers.
  • We don't sell your personal data and we don't show ads.

Who we share it with

  • The shop you order from: your name, contact details, delivery address (if any) and order, only for that order.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in the EU, Frankfurt), Netlify (app hosting), Resend (order emails), Google Fonts (font delivery). If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, that company handles the sign-in.
  • The shop's own ordering system, if the shop has connected it to Kromatch (for example Shopify or WooCommerce): your order and the contact details needed for it are passed into that system, only for shops you order from.
  • Paint makers and other businesses: only summary reports built from anonymous colour data (for example "red is the most-matched colour family in Athens this quarter"). Never personal data. [Confirm wording with your lawyer.]
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside the EU. Where they do, they use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. [Confirm per provider.]

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to how we use it. You can withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself in Settings → Delete account. For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. If you have concerns, you can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr), or the authority in your country.

If you delete your account, orders already sent to shops stay with those shops for their records but are no longer linked to you in Kromatch.

Storage on your device

Kromatch stores your settings, cart, saved details and, if you sign in, a sign-in token in your browser's storage on your device, and keeps an offline copy of the app so it works without a connection.
